PUZZLERS

Four small puzzle games

Match-3

Classic match-three, diagonal mode — only diagonal three-in-a-rows count.

Gem Match

A Kenney-gem match-three board, tuned and hardened for mobile touch play.

Pipe Flow

Tap a pipe to rotate it. Route the flow from spout to drain without leaks.

Growth Meter

A Daily Bread devotional puzzle — five short stages alternating a vine-path and a memory-match puzzle, each closing with a verse.
